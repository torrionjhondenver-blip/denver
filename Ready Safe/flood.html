🌊 Flood Preparedness

Be Prepared. Stay Safe.

What is a Flood?

A flood happens when water covers normally dry land because of heavy rain or overflowing rivers.

🌤️ Before a Flood

  • Prepare food, water, and an emergency kit.
  • Listen to weather updates.
  • Know your evacuation route.
  • Keep important documents in waterproof bags.

🌊 During a Flood

  • Move to higher ground when necessary.
  • Never walk or drive through floodwater.
  • Follow evacuation instructions.
  • Stay away from electrical wires.

🏠 After a Flood

  • Wait until authorities say it is safe to return.
  • Avoid damaged electrical equipment.
  • Drink safe water.
  • Avoid food contaminated by floodwater.

💡 Safety Advice

Always stay alert and follow official warnings. Your safety and your family's safety come first!

🔥 Fire Safety Guide

Stay alert, stay prepared, and keep your family safe.

What Is a Fire?

A fire is a dangerous event that can spread quickly and damage homes, buildings, and the environment. Fires may start because of faulty electrical wiring, unattended cooking, candles, gas leaks, or other sources of heat.

Knowing what to do before, during, and after a fire can help protect you and the people around you.

🔥 Before a Fire: Be Prepared

  • Install smoke alarms and check that they work.
  • Keep matches, lighters, and other fire-starting items away from children.
  • Never leave cooking unattended.
  • Avoid overloading electrical outlets.
  • Keep hallways, doors, and exits clear.
  • Prepare an emergency bag with essential supplies.
  • Make a family escape plan and practice it together.
  • Know two ways out of each room whenever possible.

🚨 During a Fire: Act Quickly

  • Stay calm and leave the building immediately if there is danger.
  • Warn others as you escape, but do not delay your own evacuation.
  • Use the stairs, not elevators.
  • If there is smoke, stay low and move toward a safe exit.
  • Before opening a door, check whether it feels hot. If it is hot, do not open it; find another safe exit.
  • Never return inside to get belongings or rescue pets. Tell firefighters where they may be.
  • Once outside, move away from the building and go to your family meeting place.
  • Call your local emergency number or the fire department when you are safely outside.

🏠 If You Cannot Escape

  • Go into a room away from the fire, preferably with a window.
  • Close the door between you and the fire.
  • Call emergency services and clearly tell them your location.
  • Signal for help through a window if it is safe to do so.
  • Follow instructions from emergency responders.

✅ After a Fire: Stay Safe

  • Do not re-enter the building until authorities say it is safe.
  • Stay away from damaged electrical wires and structures.
  • Do not turn on gas or electricity if you suspect damage.
  • Get medical help if anyone is injured or has trouble breathing.
  • Contact your family and let them know you are safe.
  • Follow advice from firefighters and local authorities.

💡 Important Fire Safety Reminders

Remember: Your life is more important than your belongings. If a fire spreads or you feel unsafe, leave immediately. Do not attempt to fight a large fire.

Never use water on a grease fire or a fire involving energized electrical equipment. Evacuate and call for help. Only use a fire extinguisher if the fire is small, you have been trained to use it, and you have a clear escape route.

📞 Emergency Help in the Philippines

Emergency hotline: 911

You can also contact your local Bureau of Fire Protection (BFP) station or local disaster risk reduction office. Keep important emergency numbers saved and accessible.

❤️ A Message for You and Your Family

A fire can happen unexpectedly, but preparation can make a difference. Talk with your family about where to go, how to get outside, and where to meet after escaping. Make sure children and older family members understand the plan. Practicing together can help everyone respond more confidently during an emergency.
